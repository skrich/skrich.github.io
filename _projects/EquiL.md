With colleague [Lalitha Balachandran](https://lalitha-balachandran.github.io/), I co-founded the graduate student working group Equity in Linguistics ([EquiL](https://sites.google.com/ucsc.edu/equil/home)) in Spring 2022. 

The goals of this group are to address gaps in equity within the department, in teaching, and in the field at large. The group hosts regular events including:

* First year graduate student orientation, to reveal aspects of the 'hidden curriculum' to incoming graduate students
* Peer-mentorship events to provide structured opportunities for more advanced graduate students to share their experiences with advisors and milestone requirements (e.g. qualifying papers)
* Graduate student wellness events to promote community building and resource-sharing
* Full-department workshops on topics such as evidence-based pedagogical practices and ethics in fieldwork research

The group also collects resources to be shared within the department to streamline the process of adopting new teaching and research practices.

To read more about past, current, and future projects by EquiL, you can visit their website [here](https://sites.google.com/ucsc.edu/equil/home)!

The code is simple.
Just wrap your images with `<div class="col-sm">` and place them inside `<div class="row">` (read more about the <a href="https://getbootstrap.com/docs/4.4/layout/grid/">Bootstrap Grid</a> system).
To make images responsive, add `img-fluid` class to each; for rounded corners and shadows use `rounded` and `z-depth-1` classes.
